The Person Behind Meridian

Guidance grounded in experience.

Applying to graduate school is more than completing forms and writing essays. It is about understanding your experiences, defining your direction and presenting a convincing case for why a particular program is right for you.

Meridian Admissions was created from firsthand experience navigating graduate applications, studying and working in the United States, and making the academic and career decisions that follow.

The founder's journey includes multiple graduate application cycles, a master's degree from Georgia Institute of Technology, and an MBA from the University of Georgia's Terry College of Business, supported by a full-tuition scholarship.
